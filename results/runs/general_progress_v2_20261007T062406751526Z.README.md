# Completed v2 progress-capture confirmation

This is the selected complete run: 702 unique observations and 702 saved witnesses,
covering 39 graphs × three (h,k) pairs × three repetitions × two backends.
The 39 graphs comprise 9 trees, 3 Cartesian grids, 18 Erdős–Rényi samples,
and 9 Barabási–Albert samples. The parameter pairs are (1,1), (2,1), (3,2);
repetitions r0/r1/r2 are repeated measurements of the same instance. The limit
is 30 seconds per observation. Graph-generation seeds are distinct from repeats.

Protocol: `isolated-progress-v2`; CaDiCaL versus assignment/Gurobi; symmetry off,
one Gurobi thread. Metadata records the environment and source fingerprint.
The CSV, metadata, witness JSONL, and log must stay together.
[Column/protocol definitions](../../docs/methods/progress_capture.md) and
[graph notation](../../docs/data_dictionary.md) explain the identifiers and fields.

The independent feasibility/provenance audit passed for the full dataset. No
contradictory reported optimal values or intervals were found. This is not
independent verification of UNSAT or native Gurobi certificates.

- SAT/Gurobi: 232/281 OPT observations out of 351 each; 119/70 FEASIBLE.
- OPT in all three repetitions: 77/91 out of 117 instances; 74 common.
- On 21 open instances (63 repeat pairs), better UB: SAT 39, equal 6, ILP 18.
  Better LB: SAT 0, equal 50, ILP 13. Maximum absolute UB/LB differences: 21/24.
- One Gurobi FEASIBLE observation returned normally in 4.223 seconds; its native
  stopping reason was not saved. It is neither OPT nor an external timeout.

The earlier interrupted 464-observation attempt is excluded, not merged.
V1 and v2 timing data are also kept separate because capture overhead differs.
