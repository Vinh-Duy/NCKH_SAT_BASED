# Supplemental matching-bound audit

The [version 1 audit](v1_20261017/README.md) checks whether a restricted square-coloring/matching certificate strengthens the mathematical evidence for the original 117 instances. It applies to 15 instances but improves no bound and closes no additional interval. The historical totals are unchanged.

The [method](../../../docs/methods/matching_certificates.md) and its proof explain the structural restriction and distinguish generation from independent verification. This audit does not rerun SAT or ILP and is not a new performance experiment.
