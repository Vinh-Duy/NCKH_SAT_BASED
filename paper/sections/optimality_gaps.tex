\subsection{Consolidated optimality evidence and incumbent quality}
\label{sec:optimality-gaps}
The OPT/FEASIBLE distinction describes the outcome of a particular run;
it does not, by itself, determine the quality of a FEASIBLE incumbent.
Table~\ref{tab:gap-coverage} consolidates the evidence for all
\EvidenceInstances{} graph/parameter instances, using the completed v2
confirmation before and after the addition of square-clique certificates.
The archived v1 experiment is retained separately for the observation-quality
and timeout-provenance analyses below. No v1/v2 observations or timings are pooled.
For a fixed protocol and instance $i$, let
$\ell_i^{\mathrm{th}}$ be its applicable theoretical lower bound,
$\ell_{ir}$ the recorded lower bound in observation $r$, and $u_{ir}$
the independently validated span of that observation's labeling. Combining
the six observations gives
\[
 L_i=\max\bigl\{\ell_i^{\mathrm{th}},\max_r\ell_{ir}\bigr\},
 \qquad U_i=\min_r u_{ir},
 \qquad L_i\leq\lambda_{h,k}(G_i)\leq U_i.
\]
The lower-bound inequality relies on the recorded solver bounds where
they exceed the theoretical bound; this analysis does not independently
verify UNSAT or MILP optimality certificates. The interval combines
evidence across backends and repetitions and is not attributed to a single
30-second run. The original statuses and timed coverage are unchanged.

Before adding clique bounds, \EvidenceTheory{} instances attain the
applicable theoretical lower bound and \EvidenceOtherClosed{} additional
instances have closed intervals, leaving \EvidenceOpenBefore{} open.
The archived v1 and current v2 classifications agree on all
\EvidenceVOneSame{} instances, although their interval widths and retained
progress differ. These matching counts do not imply equivalent solver progress.
The additional bound from Proposition~\ref{prop:square-clique} certifies
\EvidenceCliqueCertified{} optima from cliques and labelings alone; only
\EvidenceNewlyClosed{} of these were previously open. Combining it with
the v2 evidence therefore yields \EvidenceClosedAfter{} closed intervals
and \EvidenceOpenAfter{} open intervals. The latter remain in the ER
groups. The consolidated table is the common reference for these counts
throughout the manuscript.

For the archived v1 observation-quality analysis below, $L_i,U_i$ retain
their original v1 meaning. In particular, its \GapOpen{} open intervals
have widths \GapOpenMin{}--\GapOpenMax{}; these are not the widths or
open-case count after the v2 clique analysis.

For any stored witness of span $u$, the remaining suboptimality satisfies
\[
 u-U_i\leq u-\lambda_{h,k}(G_i)\leq u-L_i.
\]
If $L_i=U_i=u$, the witness is retrospectively known to be optimal even
when its original run returned FEASIBLE. If $u>U_i$, a strictly better
validated labeling exists, so the witness is known to be suboptimal even
if the optimum remains unresolved. Otherwise its optimality is unknown.
These are absolute bounds in label units, not confidence intervals or
the relative MIP gaps reported by a commercial solver.

In particular, \GapSATFeasibleOptimal{} SAT FEASIBLE observations contain
optimal witnesses according to the combined evidence. They remain FEASIBLE
in every original coverage and runtime table: finding an optimal labeling
and proving its optimality within a run are distinct outcomes. The analysis
does not establish why a run failed to prove optimality or isolate the cost
of proving lower bounds. Such causal conclusions require further controlled
experiments. All unresolved instances, bound sources, and observation-level
classifications are exported with source hashes under
\path{results/analysis/confirmation_gaps/}.

\paragraph{Recorded bounds versus native solver progress.}
A separate provenance audit matches the benchmark source fingerprint and
regenerates each initial greedy labeling and lower bound. On the
\BoundsOpenInstances{} open instances, all \BoundsOpenPairs{} matched
backend/repetition pairs have identical recorded lower bounds and identical
recorded upper bounds. However, both observations in all
\BoundsOpenFallbackPairs{} pairs ended with an external wall timeout and
retained the initial snapshot. The benchmark worker sends an initial incumbent
and a final result, but does not transmit intermediate search progress.
When the parent terminates a worker before its final response, any unsent
improvements are unavailable to this analysis.

Across the full cohort, all \BoundsFallbackObservations{} FEASIBLE
observations retain this initial snapshot. Consequently, the
\GapSATFeasibleOptimal{} retrospectively optimal SAT FEASIBLE witnesses were
provided by the shared initialization, not demonstrated to have been produced
by SAT search. Their validity and the combined intervals remain unchanged.
The zero bound differences on OPEN describe equality of the saved fallbacks;
they do not establish comparable native bound quality or overall solver
effectiveness. In particular, the all-three-OPT counts remain
\ConfirmSATStable{} for SAT and \ConfirmILPStable{} for ILP.
Direct comparison of bound quality at interruption requires a new protocol
that preserves intermediate primal and lower-bound updates for both backends.
Missing progress cannot be reconstructed from these stored snapshots.
