On the common solved subset, median wall time is lower for CaDiCaL at
each size (Table~\ref{tab:compact-runtime}). This must be read with the
coverage columns: at 60 vertices, SAT proves optimality in every run on
21 of 39 instances, compared with 26 for ILP. The subsecond medians
describe the jointly solved cases, not the cost of the full cohort.
The nearly flat SAT medians therefore do not demonstrate size-independent
scaling; harder instances drop out of this subset, and startup contributes
to these short measurements.
