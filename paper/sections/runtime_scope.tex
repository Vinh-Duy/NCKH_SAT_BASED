\paragraph{Scope of the runtime summaries.}
Let $S$ and $I$ be the sets of instances for which SAT and ILP,
respectively, report OPT within budget in all three repetitions.
Their intersection $J=S\cap I$ contains \ProgressJointStable{} instances,
out of $|S|=\ProgressSATStable{}$, $|I|=\ProgressILPStable{}$ and
\ProgressInstances{} instances in total. For both backends, each runtime
entry is the median, over the relevant subset of $J$, of the per-instance
median across three runs. The same instances enter both timing columns.
The overall timing subset excludes 43 instances: three satisfy the
all-three-OPT criterion for SAT only, 17 for ILP only, and 23 for neither.
``Neither'' does not rule out an OPT result in an individual repetition.
These exclusions leave out unequal coverage as well as unresolved cases;
the conditional medians cannot rank performance on the full cohort.
Full-cohort coverage and retained bounds are reported separately, and no
missing completion time is replaced by zero or by the timeout limit.
