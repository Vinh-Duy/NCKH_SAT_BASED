\subsection{Closing intervals with square-clique certificates}
\label{sec:clique-results}
We apply Proposition~\ref{prop:square-clique} post hoc to all
\CliqueInstances{} instances of the completed v2 confirmation, rather
than selecting only successful cases. For each instance, the best saved
labeling is compared with a clique bound computed on its original graph.
The independent verifier validates all \CliqueInstances{} certificate
objects. In \CliqueIndependentOptima{} cases the clique bound alone
equals the witness span, proving optimality without using the solver's
reported lower bound. Most of these cases were already closed by the
pooled solver evidence.

The clique bound improves the pooled lower bound for \CliqueImproved{}
instances and closes \CliqueNewlyClosed{} of the \ProgressOpen{}
previously open intervals. Table~\ref{tab:clique-closures} gives the two
newly certified values. For the 40-vertex graph, $|Q|=33$ implies
$\lambda_{3,2}(G)\geq2(33-1)=64$; a stored valid labeling has span 64.
For the 60-vertex graph, $|Q|=56$ gives the matching bound
$2(56-1)=110$. These equalities concern the specific generated graphs,
not every graph with the same order or edge probability.
Both selected witnesses are Gurobi repetition-0 incumbents originally
recorded as \FEAS{}; their post-hoc certification is not attributed to SAT
or counted as a Gurobi OPT result within the original time budget.

Combining the original solver intervals with this additional evidence
increases the closed-instance count from \CliqueOriginalClosed{} to
\CliqueAugmentedClosed{} out of \CliqueInstances{}, leaving
\CliqueRemainingOpen{} open. This count includes solver-dependent bounds;
it must not be confused with the \CliqueIndependentOptima{} optima certified
by clique bounds alone. Remaining intervals use
$\max\{L_{\mathrm{saved}},L_{\mathrm{clique}}\}$ and the best saved witness
span. Where the saved lower bound is stronger, its solver-dependent
evidence remains necessary.

The certificates, complete per-instance table, original-open subset,
and source fingerprints are archived under
\path{results/analysis/clique_certificates/}.
No raw status, time, or 30-second coverage figure is changed. Consequently,
these certificates strengthen the theoretical validation of selected
solutions, but do not increase either backend's timed success count or
establish performance equivalence between SAT and ILP.
