# Internal presentation outline

1. **Problem and scope** — define minimum-span $L(h,k)$-labeling on general
   simple undirected graphs; separate formulation scope from experimental
   scope.
2. **Exact/heuristic evidence** — explain feasible witnesses, lower bounds,
   optimality, UNKNOWN, and timeout semantics.
3. **Original timed study** — 39 graphs, three parameter pairs, 117 tasks,
   three operational repetitions per backend.
4. **SAT and ILP comparison** — coverage, conditional runtime, retained
   bounds, and the limits of a single overall speed ranking.
5. **Post-hoc proof closure** — 117 tasks from the original timed cohort;
   91 theory/clique, 15 capacity, and 11 replayed UNSAT certificates.
6. **Expanded six-method benchmark** — separate 69-graph cohort, 207 tasks,
   1,242 method rows, status profile, and ten-seed aggregation for the
   designated heuristic methods.
7. **Structural and heuristic results** — report structural observations and
   GA findings descriptively, without causal or universal claims.
8. **Why Phase 3** — fixed-span CNF forensics and distance-two pair audit;
   state the corrected $P(U,2)=3U+1$ count.
9. **Threshold construction** — pair equivalence, selector overhead, and
   the distinction between fewer clauses and faster search.
10. **Phase 3E** — ER-60, $(3,2)$, $U=112$ and 127; 12 valid SAT runs;
    mixed solve-time direction and unclassified $U=111$.
11. **Telemetry and Phase 3F** — intrusive/incomplete IPASIR-UP evidence;
    42 valid SAT results and six parent timeouts across 48 Phase 3F runs.
12. **Decision and limitations** — Decision D, Reference remains default,
    Threshold is secondary, no Phase 3G, and no universal performance claim.
