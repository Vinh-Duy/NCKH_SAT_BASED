## Compact manuscript tables and certificate audit

`submission_summary.tex` selects a version under `submission_summary/`.
The tables are shared by the full report and focused manuscript.
`scripts/export_submission_tables.py` checks archived input fingerprints
and computes v2 runtimes by size, family and parameter pair on the common
all-three-OPT subset. It also summarizes the historical main_r1 symmetry
measurements, with search counters restricted to complete jointly optimal
pairs. Five certificate closures are traced back to the original v2
intervals. `certificate_support.csv` separately checks mathematical support
from the saved graphs, labelings and cliques: 61 theory matches and 53 clique
matches overlap in 24 instances, giving 90 independent closures; 11 further
closures retain solver-dependent lower bounds. Each version includes metric
definitions, CSVs and an integrity manifest.
These summaries do not alter raw observations or pool the two protocols.
